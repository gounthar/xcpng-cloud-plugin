# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Jenkins Code of Conduct](https://www.jenkins.io/project/conduct/), which
governs the `jenkinsci` organisation and the wider Jenkins community. It applies here to issues,
pull requests, reviews, and commit messages.

The short version: assume good faith, disagree about the work rather than the person, and accept
that other people's constraints are real even when they are not visible to you.

## Reporting

Raise anything that needs attention with the maintainer, [@gounthar](https://github.com/gounthar),
by email at `gounthar@gmail.com`. Reports are handled privately.

The Jenkins Code of Conduct also lists the project's own reporting routes, and those apply to
conduct in Jenkins community spaces such as the community forum, mailing lists, and chat.
